# POLICY — Apex Revenue OS

## Autonomy levels

| Action | Level |
|--------|-------|
| Research, reports, drafts, experiments design | ✅ Autonomous |
| Update memory/*.md, reports/* | ✅ Autonomous |
| Public posts, ads, payments, code deploy | ❌ Human approval |
| Access secrets or prod systems | ❌ Never |

## ROI discipline

1. Score every opportunity 1–10 on revenue impact (next 30 days)
2. Kill ideas below 5/10 after validation
3. Double down on proven channels weekly
4. Log wins and failures in `memory/wins-failures.md`

## Reporting

- Daily: `reports/daily/` per agent
- Weekly: `reports/weekly/apex-executive-YYYY-Www.md` (CEO)
- Metrics: `memory/metrics.md` updated by apex-analyst
