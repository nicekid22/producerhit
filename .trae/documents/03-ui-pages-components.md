## UI — Pages & composants (MVP)

### Layouts
- Landing: navbar fixe + sections.
- Auth: card centrée.
- App: sidebar icônes (60px) + panneau paramètres (380px) + zone centrale.
- Mini-player sticky bottom.

### Composants
- `Navbar`: landing + auth.
- `Sidebar`: dashboard + library.
- `Button`: variants `primary|secondary|ghost|danger`.
- `Dropdown`, `Slider`, `Badge`.
- `Modal`: confirmation.
- `LoopCard`: lecture/progress/actions.
- `AudioPlayer`: mini-player persistant.

### States
- Loading: animations `animate-pulse`.
- Empty states: illustration simple (SVG).
- Transitions: `opacity` 200ms (page wrapper).

### Accessibilité
- Boutons et inputs avec labels.
- Focus rings visibles.
